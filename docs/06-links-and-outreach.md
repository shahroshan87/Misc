# Backlinks & Outreach Robot

## Phases
| When | Work |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | Listings: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Facebook, Nextdoor, BBB profile, Foursquare, plus the data aggregators (Whitespark in Tier 2). Hide the address wherever a site allows it. |
| Months 2–6 | Chambers; youth sports and school sponsorships; referral partners (probate, estate and divorce attorneys, CPAs, estate-sale companies, property managers, title companies); podcast guest spots; journalist platforms (Qwoted, Featured.com, Source of Sources) |
| Months 4–12 | Data PR, pitched to Candy's Dirt, DMN, Community Impact, D Magazine, Fort Worth Report, Star-Telegram, Al Día, Telemundo/Univision DFW. Stories: a monthly "DFW Foreclosure Report" from county postings; foundation repair costs by city; property-tax changes from DCAD and TAD data. |
| Ongoing | Get listed on the "best cash home buyers" lists that AI engines cite. Publish an honest "How to compare DFW cash buyers" checklist. |

## Weekly robot (Mondays)
1. Find prospects: where competitors' links come from (DataForSEO), plus local sponsorship, chamber, attorney and podcast pages.
2. Score and dedupe them into `ops/outreach/prospects.csv`, and check `suppression.csv` first.
3. Find the contact on each site's contact page.
4. Write up to 15 personal **Gmail drafts** labeled `Outreach/Pending`. You press Send.
5. Draft one follow-up after 7 days. Sort replies, and honor opt-outs immediately.
6. Email you a weekly summary.

## Guardrails
- **Drafts only.** The robot reads other people's websites, so it never gets the ability to send.
- Never pay for links or ask for "dofollow".
- Every email includes the PO Box address and an opt-out line (CAN-SPAM).
- Never email competitors or personal addresses.
- DataForSEO spend is capped at $1 or less per run.
