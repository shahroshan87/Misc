# DFW Competitor Analysis ("sell my house fast" / "we buy houses")

_Researched 2026-09-24. This comes from 43 search-result sets covering 33 DFW cities. The network policy blocked fetching competitor sites directly, so on-page details (forms, word counts, schema, review counts, the live Map Pack) still need a hands-on check. Positions are approximate._

**Nationals:** Opendoor, Offerpad, HomeGo, HomeVestors and sellmyhousefast.com. Opendoor's city pages show real market data (median days to sell, % sold in 30 days, median price).

**Directories cited by AI engines:** Clever (listwithclever.com and cleveroffers.com), HomeLight, Houzeo, RealEstateBees, HelloReeve, CashHomeBuyersCrew, iBuyer.com, Bankrate, Yelp, BBB, Zillow profiles.

## Table stakes
- Cash offer in 24h, close in about 7 days.
- As-is, no fees or commissions, closing costs paid.
- The seller picks the date.
- Form plus local phone number.
- One page per suburb.

## Gaps we exploit
1. No local investor shows local data, neighborhoods or ZIPs on city pages.
2. No one publishes offer math. Directories say "30–70% of value".
3. Proof is vague: no counts, case studies, or named title companies.
4. No DFW-specific distress content: clay-soil foundations, hail roofs, cast-iron plumbing.
5. 2026 soft market: rising days on market and price cuts. Nobody targets "listing expired / house won't sell".
6. No Spanish-language competitor pages found.
7. Local buyers are missing from third-party "best of" lists.
8. Technical sloppiness is common.

## City competitiveness
- **Easiest:** Little Elm, Prosper, Midlothian, Wylie, Cedar Hill, Waxahachie, Euless, Grapevine, Weatherford, Cleburne; outer counties (Ellis, Johnson, Parker, Kaufman).
