# Owner Guide: Week 0 Checklist (plain English)

This takes about 2–3 hours in total, and you can do it over a few days. Tell me in chat when each step is done, or if you get stuck.

## 1. Find where your domain is registered (10 min)
1. Search your email for "homeoffersolutions.com" plus words like "renewal", "invoice" or "domain".
2. If you find nothing, the domain was probably bought through Carrot. Log in to Carrot and check **Settings → Domains**.
3. Tell me three things: **where it's registered**, **the expiry date**, and **whether you can log in there**.

## 2. Carrot (15 min). Do NOT cancel yet.
1. Log in and check whether you're still being billed.
2. Export your old leads (Carrot → Leads → Export).
3. Write down the phone number shown on the old site, and check whether it's a Carrot or CallRail "tracking number". If it is, it stops working when Carrot is cancelled, so we'll choose a new permanent number first.

## 3. Email on the domain (5 min)
Do you receive email at an @homeoffersolutions.com address? Yes or no. If yes, we copy its settings before moving anything, so email keeps working.

## 4. Google Business Profile (10 min)
1. Go to business.google.com and sign in with every Google account you might have used before.
2. If a "Home Offer Solutions" profile exists, **don't create a new one**. We'll reclaim the old one, because duplicate profiles get suspended.
3. Also check search.google.com/search-console to see whether the old site is listed there.

## 5. Free accounts (30 min; I'll walk you through each one live)
- Cloudflare (hosting)
- Google Search Console and GA4
- Bing Webmaster Tools
- Pushover (phone app, about $5, for instant lead alerts)
- A new Google Sheet named "HOS Leads"

## 6. Business facts (15 min): reply with
- Your legal business name, or DBA. If "Home Offer Solutions" isn't your legal name, you'll need a Texas assumed-name certificate from your county clerk (about $25).
- The public phone number and your business hours.
- The city your home is in. We won't publish it; it tells us where the map pack can realistically rank.
- The counties you buy in.
- The title companies you close with.
- The year you started, and roughly how many houses you've bought.
- Whether you or your team take Spanish-language calls (you said yes).

## 7. Proof folder (60 min)
Create a Google Drive folder named "HOS Proof" and share it with me. Put in:
- **A deal list:** for each deal, the city, the neighborhood, the situation (inherited, foreclosure, repairs…), days to close, and numbers only if you're comfortable sharing them.
- **Photos** of each house (before and after if you have them). On iPhone, go to Settings → Camera → Formats → "Most Compatible".
- **Reviews:** links or screenshots, with the seller's okay to use them on the site.
- **Team photos:** real people, no stock photos.

## 8. City notes (by voice, about 5 min per city)
For each city you've bought in, record a voice memo or fill in the short form I'll send:
- Which neighborhoods have you bought in, and what were those houses like?
- What problems keep showing up there (foundation, roofs, old plumbing, tenants, code issues)?
- Is there a seller story from that city you're allowed to share?
- Anything a local would know that an outsider wouldn't?

## 9. Allow network access for the robots (5 min)
Open the cloud environment menu in the session title bar → **Edit** → **Network access**, and add these domains:
- `api.dataforseo.com`
- `ssl.bing.com`
- `web.archive.org`
- `homeoffersolutions.com`
- `www.homeoffersolutions.com`
- `*.workers.dev`

Optionally, also add the competitor sites listed in docs/01 so I can study them directly.

## 10. Choose Tier 1 or Tier 2 (see docs/03)
There's no rush. The website is the same either way.

## Your ongoing routine (about 1–2 hours a week)
- Respond to lead alerts fast. Speed to lead wins deals.
- After every closing, text the seller your Google review link. I'll give you a template and a QR code.
  - Never offer anything in exchange for a review.
  - Never ask sellers to mention a staff member.
- Record **2 short phone videos a month**: a house walk-through, or you answering one seller question.
- Send the outreach drafts in Gmail (10–15 minutes a week).
- Reply "APPROVE" or send corrections on preview emails.
