## City pages without penalties
- Launch in waves, and check indexing before starting the next wave.
- Unique proof on every page: deals, reviews, owner notes, local stats with sources.
- County facts go on county pages. City pages get city-level, first-hand material.
- Never build a city × situation page matrix.
- Never let AI invent "local facts".

## Local SEO
- **Whitespark 2026 ranking factors:** GBP (Google Business Profile) 32%, reviews 20%, on-page 15%. A new "AI visibility" factor rewards "best of" list inclusion. [V/S]
- **Reviews:** the ranking bump comes at about 10 reviews. After that, recency and review text matter more. [V, Sterling Sky]
- **Hidden addresses:** service-area businesses with a hidden address rank worse than those showing a real address. [V]
- **Suspension waves (Apr–May 2026):** the top trigger was keyword-stuffed business names. [S]
- **Video verification** is now the default. [V/S]
- **April 2026 review policy:** no incentives, no review gating, no asking customers to name a staff member, no employee quotas. [S]

## Technical
- FAQ rich results are gone (May 2026). FAQPage markup is still harmless. [V]
- **Core Web Vitals targets:** LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS ≤ 0.1. [K]
- Most AI crawlers don't execute JavaScript, so use static HTML. [K]
- IndexNow feeds Bing and Yandex, not Google. [K]
- Don't use self-serving review stars. [K]

## Links
- A few dozen relevant local referring domains usually wins local lead-gen.
- Sources for those links:
  - chambers of commerce
  - sponsorships
  - referral partners: attorneys, CPAs, estate-sale companies, property managers, title companies
  - journalist platforms: Qwoted, Featured.com, Source of Sources
  - local data PR
- Never buy links or use PBNs.
