What this changes in the plan:
- **The old site is effectively down**, and probably has been since early 2025. The plan assumed a live Carrot site to migrate from and keep as a 30-day fallback. There is no fallback, and little live ranking left to protect.
- **Migration shrinks.** The redirect map still gets built from Search Console and the Wayback Machine, but it is a small job, and "don't cancel Carrot yet" no longer applies if the account is confirmed closed.
- **Speed matters more than sequencing.** Every week without a working site is another week the domain sends Google errors. A short, honest holding page on the new host is worth putting up as soon as the phone number and business name are confirmed.
- **Tier 2 email is already in place.** Workspace is active on the domain, so that line in the cost table is not a new cost.
- **The Business Profile is a reclaim-and-revive job**, not a new listing.

Still unknown: whether the old site's phone number was a Carrot tracking number, and what the old site's pages were. Reading the Wayback Machine needs `web.archive.org` allowed in the environment's network settings.

## 4. Strategy re-check: 2026-06-10 to 2026-11-10

Findings come from search-result summaries of Google's pages, trade press and video listings. Nothing here was read at the primary source, and no video was watched.
